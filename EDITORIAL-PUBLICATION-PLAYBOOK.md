# INEXTGROUP: First Public Editorial Posts — Publication Standard
Date: 2026-11-01

## Why the site has no entries in the post sitemap
The latest screenshot shows HTTP 200 and zero <loc> entries at /sitemap.xml, with seven entries at /sitemap-pages.xml. That does NOT prove the count of published Blogger posts, but is consistent with a site containing only published Pages and no indexed post URLs. Review the audit JSON's publishedPostsCount and publishedPagesCount before concluding.

## What to publish first (manual editorial workflow)
1. Finish and verify website ownership, contact and policy text first. Do not publish policy placeholders or claims about tracking tools not actually configured.
2. Prepare a small set of useful original entertainment guides that do not require TMDB licensing or reuse of unauthorized posters. Suitable topics include how to find legitimate official streaming platforms in a specific country (verify sources), how movie ratings work (cite public source), and a transparent guide to navigating INEXTGROUP. These are suggestions, not prewritten facts.
3. Write each article from verified primary sources, with unique observations and clear source/date attribution. Use blogger/editorial-post-template.html as a structural template only. Remove all placeholders before publication.
4. Label posts accurately: only use Movies, TV Shows, Trending or genre labels if actually applicable. Do not tag every article Trending.
5. Set a unique Blogger Search Description, descriptive title, original/cleared feature image and descriptive alt text if applicable.
6. Preview on a narrow mobile viewport. Review title, links, author/update text, footer pages and navigation.
7. Publish only when source rights, editorial quality and business policies are clear. Then rerun the published-post audit and check /sitemap.xml.
8. Use Google Search Console URL Inspection for the actual published URL. Discovery and indexing are not guaranteed.

## Current hard stops
- TMDB_RIGHTS_REVIEWED=no, so commercial API-based metadata and artwork must not be auto-published.
- Do not publish fictional Blogger API tests.
- Do not insert ad scripts, paid partner offers or affiliate URLs until those actual programs are active and policy-ready.
- No live access or proof of AdSense approval exists.

A legitimate original article appears in Blogger Published Posts, the public page renders correctly, the post sitemap lists an actual accessible URL when Blogger has updated, and Search Console can inspect that page. If Sitemap remains empty, review Blogger visibility settings, custom robots configuration and published-post inventory before changing anything.

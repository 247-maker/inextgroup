Editorial and Corrections Policy

INEXTGROUP is an independent movie and television information website. Draft — review and replace every bracketed field before publication.

Sources and attribution

We use source-attributed entertainment metadata and verified official information. Ratings shown as TMDB ratings are community ratings provided by that source, not our own review scores. We distinguish confirmed details from expected or reported information.

Human review

Automated tools may prepare drafts, but substantive articles are reviewed before publication. We do not publish fake quotes, fabricated reviews, unverified streaming availability or mass-produced near-identical pages solely for search traffic.

Corrections

Send the article URL, proposed correction and a supporting source to [PUBLIC CONTACT EMAIL]. Material corrections should be reflected in the page and, where appropriate, noted with an update date.

Commercial independence

Ads and affiliate links are labelled; commercial relationships do not automatically change factual metadata or ratings.
